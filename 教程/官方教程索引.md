## 六、推荐学习路径（按时间顺序）

```
Week 1-2  ───→  Embedded-Design-Tutorials/ZynqMPSoC-EDT  (Ch.1-5)
                阶段1：环境 + 阶段2：Vivado + 阶段3：Vitis 裸机

Week 3    ───→  Vivado-Design-Tutorials/IP_Integrator
                阶段2：IPI 深入 + 版本控制

Week 4-5  ───→  Embedded-Design-Tutorials/ZynqMPSoC-EDT  (Ch.6-9)
                阶段4：PetaLinux + 阶段3：FreeRTOS

Week 6    ───→  Vitis-Tutorials/Embedded_Software/Getting_Started
                阶段3：Vitis Unified IDE

Week 7-8  ───→  Vitis-Tutorials/Getting_Started/Vitis_HLS
                Vitis-Tutorials/Developer_Contributed/03-HLS_Code_Optimization
                阶段5：Vitis HLS

Week 9-10 ───→  Vitis-AI-Tutorials (branch 3.5) RESNET18 + PyTorch
                阶段6：Vitis AI 量化部署

Week 11   ───→  Vitis-Tutorials/Vitis_Platform_Creation/02-Edge-AI-ZCU104
                阶段6：自定义 DPU 平台

Week 12+  ───→  阶段7-10：高速接口 + RTOS + 项目实战
```
